```markdown
## Decision header

At most five lines, at the very top of every gate file (CONFIG § Group approval):

**Changes and Additions:** one line.
**User Review:** the load-bearing claims or edits, named — the two or three things a reviewer should actually open.
**Filing Target:** where the set files, what becomes canon.

The body follows. The header is what the For-You card surfaces; it exists so a
review is a decision, not a rubber stamp.
```
