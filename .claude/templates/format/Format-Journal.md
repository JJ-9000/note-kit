# YYYY-MM-DD-kebab-slug

Personal prose in your own words. When it runs to more than one topic, cluster it under **bold headers drawn from your own language**, never a prescribed heading. Separate sub-thoughts with a blank line so each renders as its own line. Write toward understanding, not a deliverable; no editorial summary or headline.

**Decisions** / **Action items** / **Open questions**

Optional, and only when the reflection contains them: what you settled, what you committed to, what you left open, each in your own phrasing.
