# Title-Case-Hyphens

One sentence: what this source is and why it is cited.

## Bibliographic record

- **Title:** Full title of the source.
- **Author:** Author or organization.
- **Date:** Publication date (YYYY-MM-DD or YYYY if only year is known).
- **URL:** Direct link to the source.
- **Accessed:** YYYY-MM-DD.

## Relevant excerpt

The verbatim passage or the paraphrase that is load-bearing for the parent note. Quote with quotation marks; paraphrase without. Limit to what the parent actually relies on.

## Context

One to three sentences on how this source connects to the parent: what claim it supports, disputes, or contextualizes.
